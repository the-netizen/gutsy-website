The Science

Why 30 Plants Per Week?

The American Gut Project studied over 10,000 people and found that those who ate 30+ different plants per week had significantly more diverse gut microbiomes.

🦠

Greater Diversity

More plant varieties feed more types of gut bacteria, creating a richer, more resilient microbiome ecosystem.

🛡️

Stronger Immunity

Over 70% of your immune system lives in your gut. A diverse microbiome means better immune function and disease resistance.

⚡

Better Digestion

Different plants provide different fibres that nourish different microbes, improving digestion and nutrient absorption.

Based on research from the American Gut Project (McDonald et al., 2018)
